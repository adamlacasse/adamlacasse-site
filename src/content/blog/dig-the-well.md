"Don't dig the well when you're thirsty." - Chinese Proverb?

I've asked myself why I'm even building this site. I have a job; I'm not really in the market for a new one.
I don't exactly have a lot of time on my hands.

I guess there are three reasons:

1. I love learning and building.
2. AI has automated the tedium and boilerplate from coding.
3. Who knows what will be efficacious in the future? Certainly not me.
